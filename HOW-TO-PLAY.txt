SKIP STONE - HOW TO PLAY
========================

Double-click the "Skip Stone" icon on your desktop.
Click "Pick up a stone" to start.


GOAL
----
Throw a flat stone across the lake and make it skip as many times
as you can. The further it goes, the better.


1. THROW
--------
- PRESS AND HOLD (mouse button, finger, or SPACE bar).
- A "Power" bar fills up and empties again, over and over.
- LET GO when it's full. It turns gold and says "Now!".
  More power = a longer first throw.


2. SKIP
-------
While the stone flies, look at the water ahead of it:
- A small GOLD ring shows where the stone will land.
- A big WHITE ring shrinks toward it.
- TAP (click, touch, or SPACE) when the white ring reaches the
  gold ring, right as the stone touches the water.

  "Perfect!"     = great timing. The stone stays fast and bounces high.
  "Skip!"        = good timing. It skips, but loses some speed.
  "Just in time" = a tiny bit late, but it still skips.
  "Too early" or "Too late" = the stone sinks.

You get ONE tap per bounce, so don't tap too early.


3. THE END
----------
Each skip is smaller and quicker than the last. When the stone runs
out of bounce, it glides to a stop. You'll see your skips, distance
and number of perfects. Your best distance is saved.


SOUND
-----
The game has sound: lake waves, birds, the wind-up, splashes, a
sparkle on perfect skips, a plunk when the stone sinks, and a little
tune when you beat your best.
- Sound starts after your first click or tap (browsers require this).
- The "Sound on / Sound off" button at the top right mutes it.


TIPS
----
- Let go when the power bar is gold.
- Watch the rings, not the stone.
- The last few bounces come very fast. Keep your rhythm.
- Distance posts in the water show every 10 metres.
