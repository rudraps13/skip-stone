SKIP STONE - HOW TO THROW FURTHER THAN 37 METRES
================================================

If your throws keep ending at about 37 m, the stone is SINKING on
its first touch of the water. It isn't skipping at all.
37-45 m is only how far the throw itself carries.
To go further, you have to make it SKIP.


1. THROW AT FULL POWER
----------------------
Hold down. Watch the power bar.
Let go when it turns GOLD and says "now".


2. DON'T TAP STRAIGHT AFTER THROWING
------------------------------------
This is the most common mistake!
You only get ONE tap per bounce.
If you tap while the stone is still high in the air, it counts as
"Too early" and the stone sinks.


3. WATCH THE RINGS ON THE WATER, NOT THE STONE
----------------------------------------------
- A small GOLD ring appears on the water where the stone will land.
- A big WHITE ring shrinks toward it.
- TAP the moment the white ring reaches the gold ring.
  That's when the stone touches the water.


4. KEEP TAPPING FOR EVERY BOUNCE
--------------------------------
After each skip, a NEW pair of rings appears further ahead.
Tap again each time.
The bounces get faster and faster, so keep your rhythm.


WHAT THE MESSAGES MEAN
----------------------
"Too early"        -> tap a little LATER next time
"Plunk. Too late"  -> tap a little SOONER next time
"Skip"             -> good timing
"Perfect"          -> great timing! The stone keeps more speed
                      and goes much further


WHAT YOU CAN REACH
------------------
No skips        ->  about 40 m
Good timing     ->  about 6 skips, 110 m
Perfect timing  ->  12+ skips, 200 m or more
