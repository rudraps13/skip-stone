# Skip Stone

A one-button game about skipping stones across a lake at sunset.

**Play:** open `index.html` in any browser, or visit the GitHub Pages link for this repository.

- **Hold** to wind up, **let go** to throw.
- **Tap** just as the stone touches the water, when the white ring shrinks onto the gold one.
- A perfect tap keeps the stone fast and high. A late or early tap and it sinks.

A mountain lake at sunset, with water that reflects the sky, sun glitter, spray and ripples. Sound effects are made live in the browser with the Web Audio API: lake waves, birdsong, splashes and a plunk.

Works with a mouse, a touchscreen, or the Space bar. Your best distance is saved in your browser.

Made with HTML and JavaScript in a single file. No install, no build step.
